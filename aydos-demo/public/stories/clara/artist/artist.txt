[1] Clara’s university art club was preparing for its annual student exhibition. Paintings were being hung on the walls, sculptures were being arranged on tables, and small cards were being placed beside each work. Clara had volunteered to help because her friend Mina was exhibiting a large watercolor painting of a rainy city street. By six o’clock, almost everything had been prepared. The gallery was supposed to be opened to visitors the next morning.

[2] Clara was carrying a box of programs when she heard a crash from the other side of the room. Mina’s painting had fallen from the wall. Worse, a cup of black coffee had been knocked over, and a dark stain now covered one corner of the painting. “How did this happen?” Mina cried. Nobody knew. The painting had been hung securely only an hour earlier.

[3] While the other students tried to clean the watercolor, Clara noticed several small paw prints on the floor. She followed them behind a curtain and discovered Yakkai hiding underneath a table. “Yakkai!” she whispered. He must have climbed into her bag before she left home. When Clara tried to grab him, he got frightened and ran straight through the gallery. A sculpture got knocked over, two students got splashed with cleaning water, and Yakkai disappeared through an open door.

[4] Fortunately, the sculpture wasn’t damaged, and Yakkai was eventually caught by the security guard. The painting, however, could not be completely restored. Clara felt terrible. She explained what had happened and offered to pay for the damage, but Mina suddenly began laughing. The strange black stain looked surprisingly beautiful against the painted rain.

[5] The next morning, the exhibition was opened as planned. Mina decided to display the damaged painting without hiding the stain. To everyone’s surprise, it was admired by dozens of visitors. One professor even said the accidental mark made the painting more interesting. Yakkai, meanwhile, was kept safely at home.

When Clara returned that evening, she found him asleep on her bed.

“You nearly got me banned from the art club,” she told him.

Yakkai opened one eye. He did not look sorry.
