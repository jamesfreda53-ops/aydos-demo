[1] Grey, heavy clouds bubbled across the sky like dark smoke. The wind howled across the Caribbean sea, whipping the water into angry white peaks. A massive hurricane was forecast to hit the coast by late afternoon, giving Finn and his older sister, Isla, only a few hours. Clinging to the side of their rocking motorboat, they checked their masks. They were hunting for the Black Horizon—a legendary pirate ship that had vanished three centuries ago.

[2] They plunged into the choppy water. Beneath the surface, the sea was crowded and restless. Schools of silver fish darted frantically through forests of swaying sea grass, and a large sea turtle glided past them into the deep, sensing the oncoming storm. Then, looming out of the sandy seabed, they saw it. The dark, splintered shape of a massive wooden hull lay swallowed by the ocean floor, looking like a sunken cage.

[3] Squeezing through a rotting gap in the ship's side, they entered the captain's cabin. It was cold, dark, and eerie. Inside, sitting majestically in a grand wooden chair, was a human skeleton wearing a tattered, faded blue coat. A rusted cutlass sword lay across its bony lap.

Isla noticed that the skeleton's bony fingers were wrapped tightly around a small, heavy iron box. Despite Finn's warning look, she gently nudged the skeleton's hand. With a sudden snap, the ancient bones crumbled into grey dust, and the box fell into Isla's hands.

[4] But the movement disturbed the dark water. Out of the shadows of the cabin, a sleek, grey reef shark glided into view. Its black, empty eyes locked onto them, and it bared rows of razor-sharp teeth. Panic surged through them. Finn and Isla kicked their flippers frantically, scrambling out of the wreck just as the shark lunged forward. 

[5] They burst through the surface into the howling wind, desperately climbing back onto their tossing boat just in time. Finn collapsed onto the deck, pulling off his mask and gasping for air. "Are you crazy?" he yelled over the roar of the wind. "That shark almost had us for lunch!"

[6] Isla laughed, though her hands were shaking as she held up the heavy, rusted metal. "Maybe, but look what we got! It's real, Finn. The legend was real." "We need to start the engine and get back to land before the storm hits," Finn urged, eyeing the dark sky. "We can look at that later!"

[7] "Just one second," Isla insisted. Safe on deck, with the storm clouds darkening the sky, Isla pulled out a small pocketknife. She slid the blade under the rusted lock of the iron box and pried it upward. With a loud crack, the lid began to lift. Finn leaned in, holding his breath as a strange, warm light peeked through the gap…
