[1] Arthur and Gemma stood inside the kitchen of the lunar dome, surrounded by floating metal spoons. Today was Arthur’s thirteenth birthday, and they wanted to bake a chocolate cake to celebrate. Arthur carefully measured the flour, whilst Gemma used a wire whisk to mix the eggs. "In lunar gravity, we must be very precise with our measurements," Gemma said, tapping her spoon.

[2] Arthur mixed the ingredients to create a thick, sweet batter. He poured it into a baking tin and slid it into the oven. However, they made a mistake: they forgot to turn on the machine that made gravity in the kitchen. As the oven heated up, the air bubbles in the mixture began to expand quickly in the weightless room.

[3] Through the oven's glass window, Gemma’s eyes went wide with shock. Instead of rising flat, the chocolate cake was growing like a giant bubble. It pushed through the oven door, emerging into the room as a warm, floating sphere. "Arthur, look!" Gemma shouted, pointing at the sweet, brown ball. "It’s escaping!"

[4] The chocolate ball floated slowly into the centre of the room. Arthur grabbed a plastic spatula, whilst Gemma held a big bowl to use as a net. "Be careful!" Arthur warned, ducking as a small blob of warm chocolate flew past his ear. If the cake hit the ventilation holes in the wall, it would block the air system of the dome.

[5] The cake floated out of the kitchen and into the long corridor, propelled by the soft wind from the air vents. The two  children jumped after it in slow motion, pushing off the walls. Gemma tried to catch it with her bowl but missed, spinning around in the air. Arthur tried to stop it, but the cake bounced off his chest, leaving a brown mark on his white jumpsuit.

[6] We have to trap it before it reaches the control room!" Gemma gasped. She saw a large, clean plastic crate tied to the wall. She quickly untied it and threw the open box to Arthur. Together, they floated on each side of the giant chocolate mass, gently pushing it inside the container.

[7] With a soft click, Gemma closed the lid, trapping the unruly cake inside. Both children sank to the floor, breathing heavily and covered in crumbs, before they started to laugh. Gemma licked a stray bit of chocolate off her finger and smiled. "Well," she grinned, "it looks messy, but the taste is absolutely out of this world!"
