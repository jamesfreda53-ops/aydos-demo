[1] For the last hour, Chloe had stared out of the train window, watching the landscape change. The train rattled past rolling hills covered in golden leaves, and empty brown fields bordered by old stone walls. Every click-clack of the train tracks felt like a countdown. Inside her jacket, she held Pip tightly. He was a small, friendly terrier with fluffy brown ears and bright, curious eyes, but even his warm weight couldn't stop her hands from trembling.

It wasn't just the chilly autumn wind making her shiver. Out here, a deep, peaceful stillness lay over the misty valley. There were no cars, only a few winding footpaths and narrow dirt lanes. With her mum so ill in hospital, Chloe felt completely lost. She was going to live with an aunt and uncle she barely remembered in this quiet countryside, and on Monday, she had to face a brand-new school. A heavy knot of dread sat in her stomach. What if they don't like me? she worried. What if I spend the whole year completely alone?

[2] The train let out a loud hiss of steam as it groaned to a halt at Great Oak Station. Chloe stepped down onto the wooden platform, her heart hammering against her ribs.

"Chloe! Over here, dear!" 

A woman in a bright yellow woollen coat was waving energetically, beside a tall man with a kind, wrinkled face. But Chloe’s eyes fell on a third person: a girl her own age wearing a bright red scarf. The girl was holding a thick sketchbook, clutching it to her chest just like Chloe was holding Pip.

[3] "Welcome, Chloe," Aunt Sarah said, pulling her into a warm hug that smelled of lavender and rain.

[4] The girl in the red scarf stepped forward. "Hi, I'm Daisy. Your cousin."

"I... I didn't know I had a cousin here," Chloe admitted, her voice quiet.

"Surprise!" Daisy smiled. Then, she noticed a black charcoal smudge on Chloe’s thumb, and the corner of a notebook poking out of Chloe’s backpack. "Wait, do you draw?"

[5] Chloe looked down, blushing. "Yes. I sketch mythical creatures. Mostly dragons."

[6] Daisy's eyes lit up. She flipped open her own sketchbook. "No way! Me too! Look, I'm drawing a griffin."

She showed Chloe a beautiful, detailed pencil drawing of a creature with an eagle's head and a lion's body. For the first time all day, the heavy knot in Chloe's stomach dissolved, replaced by a sudden, warm spark of excitement. "That's incredible! I have some dragon designs we can look at."

[7] "We have so much to talk about," Daisy grinned, gently taking Chloe's suitcase. "Come on, let's get you home."

As they walked to the car, the chilly autumn air did not feel quite so cold anymore.
